ELZKII JOKI - FIREBASE SETUP

1. Firebase Console > Authentication > Sign-in method:
   - Enable Email/Password
   - Enable Google
- Email/password accounts must verify their email before login is accepted
2. Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains:
   - Add your deployed domain (example: your-project.vercel.app)
3. Google Cloud OAuth client:
   - Authorized JavaScript origins: https://YOUR-DOMAIN
   - Confirm OAuth client ID matches the one in ej-auth.js.
4. Realtime Database > Rules:
   - Open FIREBASE_RULES.json, copy its contents, paste into Rules, then Publish.
5. Deploy the complete contents of this ZIP together in one directory.
